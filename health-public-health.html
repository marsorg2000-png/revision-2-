Key GCSE Revision Takeaways: Factors Prompting Change

Epidemics & Crisis The Black Death (1348) and recurring Cholera outbreaks (1831, 1848, 1853, 1866) forced authorities into action. The Great Stink of 1858 directly affected politicians in Parliament, leading to funding for Bazalgette's London sewer system.
The Vote & Politics The 1867 Reform Act gave working-class men the vote. Politicians suddenly had to win working-class approval, prompting Disraeli's government to pass the compulsory 1875 Public Health Act and later the Liberal social reforms.
National Security & War During the 1899 Boer War, recruiting officers were alarmed to find 40% of volunteers physically unfit for service. This military fear, coupled with Booth and Rowntree's poverty reports, ended the era of laissez-faire once and for all.
