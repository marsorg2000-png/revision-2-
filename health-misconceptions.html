Summary Checklist for GCSE Cold War Exams

1. Check the Leaders:

Always align the event with the correct superpower leaders. Truman & Stalin governed the 1948 Blockade; Kennedy & Khrushchev faced the 1961 Wall and 1962 Cuba Crisis; Johnson & Brezhnev led during the 1968 Prague Spring.

2. Distinguish Immediate vs Long-Term:

The Bay of Pigs was an immediate 1961 tactical disaster for the CIA, but its long-term consequence was convincing Castro that an American invasion was imminent, leading directly to the installation of Soviet nuclear missiles.

3. Warsaw Pact Membership is Critical:

Remember why Hungary was brutally invaded in 1956 (Nagy tried to withdraw from the Warsaw Pact and declare neutrality), whereas in 1968 Dubček explicitly insisted Czechoslovakia would stay inside the Warsaw Pact, yet was invaded under the Brezhnev Doctrine.
